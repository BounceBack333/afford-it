Their Story

Privacy Policy. Last updated: November 8, 2026

Their Story records the people you love answering questions about their lives. This policy explains what happens to those recordings and the other information you put in the app.

What we collect

Nothing. We don't run servers for this app, we don't have accounts, and we can't hear or read your recordings.

Where your recordings are stored

Recordings, transcripts, names and photos are stored on your iPhone. If you're signed in to iCloud, they're also stored in your own private iCloud account so they sync between your devices. Apple handles that storage under Apple's privacy policy. We have no access to it.

Microphone and speech recognition

The microphone is used only while you record a story. After a recording, the app can turn it into text using Apple's speech recognition. On most iPhones this happens on the device. For some languages, Apple processes the audio on Apple's servers under Apple's privacy policy. You can turn speech recognition off for Their Story in iPhone Settings; recordings still work without it.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily question, the app schedules a local notification on your iPhone. No notification servers are involved.

Sharing

Audio files, text and story card videos leave the app only when you share them, to whoever you send them to. The app's export writes files to the app's own folder on your iPhone.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time purchase are processed by Apple. We receive no payment details. The app checks with Apple whether your purchase is active.

Tracking and ads

The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not share data with third parties.

Children

The app is for adults recording family stories. It does not knowingly collect information from children.

Deleting your data

Delete a story or a person inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything on your iPhone. Recordings synced to iCloud are removed from iCloud when you delete them in the app.
