Common questions

Who is it for? Anyone with a parent, grandparent, partner or friend whose stories they would miss. It also works for recording your own.

How does recording work? The app asks one question from a book of 300. Hand them the phone, or ask out loud and let them talk. Tap the red button to start and the square to stop. Recording keeps going if the screen locks.

Where do the recordings go? They are stored on your iPhone. If you are signed in to iCloud, they also sync to your other devices through your own iCloud account. Nothing is sent to us.

How do transcripts work? After you stop, the app writes down what was said using Apple's speech recognition, on the iPhone when your language supports it. You can edit the words at any time. If no words come through, the recording is still saved.

What is a story card? A short vertical video of one story: the question, their name, a moving waveform and the words as they say them. Share it to Messages, Instagram, TikTok or anywhere else.

What is free? Your first 3 stories, playing them back, and exporting them. Premium adds unlimited stories, transcripts for every story and story cards. Plans: $4.99 a month, $29.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or $79.99 once. Prices may differ outside the United States.

Can I get my recordings out? Yes, always, free or not. Settings → Export writes every recording as an audio file plus a text file of every word. Save them to Files, AirDrop them or email them. The same files also appear in the Files app under Their Story.

How do I cancel? Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone.
