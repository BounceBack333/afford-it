Common questions

What can I save? Notes, voice notes, photos and screenshots, PDFs and other documents, links, emails and your calendar. From any app, tap Share, then Remember.

How do answers work? When you ask a question, Remember finds the memories that match it, then Apple's on-device language model reads them and writes a short answer. Each fact has a number that opens the memory it came from.

Which iPhones get written answers? iPhones that support Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro and later) with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. On other iPhones, Ask shows the closest matching memories instead, and everything else works the same.

Is anything uploaded? No. Search, reading text in photos, speech to text and answers all run on the iPhone. There are no accounts and no servers. Your memories are included in your encrypted iPhone backup.

The share sheet doesn't show Remember. In the share sheet, scroll the row of apps to the end, tap More, and turn Remember on. Items you share appear the next time you open the app.

What is free? Your first 25 memories, with unlimited questions about them. Premium keeps going: $9.99 a month, $49.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or $129.99 once. Prices may differ outside the United States.

Can I get my data out? Yes, always. Settings → Export everything makes a zip of every file plus a text file of every word.

How do I cancel? Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone.
