Remember

Privacy Policy. Last updated: November 10, 2026

Remember keeps the notes, recordings, photos, documents and links you save, and answers questions about them. This policy explains what happens to that information.

What we collect

Nothing. We don't run servers for this app, there are no accounts, and we can't see anything you save.

Where your memories are stored

On your iPhone, inside the app's own storage. Like your other apps, they are included in your iPhone backup if backups are on (to iCloud or your computer, under your Apple settings). The app itself doesn't sync them to any cloud service, and we never receive them.

How answers are made

Searching, reading text in photos and documents, and finding names all use Apple frameworks that run on the iPhone. Written answers and summaries come from Apple's on-device language model (Apple Intelligence), which runs on the iPhone and does not send your memories to Apple or to us.

Microphone and speech recognition

The microphone is used only while you record a voice note. After a recording, the app turns it into text with Apple's speech recognition, on the iPhone only. The app never uses Apple's server-based recognition: if this iPhone can't recognize your language on the device, the recording is kept without a transcript and you can type what was said. You can turn speech recognition off for Remember in iPhone Settings; recordings still work without it.

Calendar

Off unless you turn it on. When on, the app reads your events from the last 90 days and the next 60 so you can ask about them. Events are copied into the app on your iPhone and never sent anywhere.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you scan paper. Photos are read when you pick them or share them to Remember. If you use Screenshots, the app reads your recent screenshots on the iPhone, keeps a copy of the ones that contain text, and ignores the rest. You can turn off "Keep up with new screenshots" at any time, or remove photo access in iPhone Settings.

Heads-ups, widgets and iPhone search

Dates found in what you save, the widgets, and results in iPhone search are all produced on the iPhone from your own memories. The widgets read a small file the app keeps on the device. On the Lock Screen, event names and saved details are hidden until you unlock. The iPhone search index uses complete file protection, so it can't be read while the iPhone is locked. The morning brief shows only "Your morning brief" when notification previews are hidden. Siri works without Face ID or a passcode. If you'd rather Siri not read answers while the iPhone is locked, turn on "Only answer Siri when unlocked" in Remember's Settings.

Share extension

When you share something to Remember from another app, it is saved into the app's storage on your iPhone. Nothing else is read from the other app.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time purchase are processed by Apple. We receive no payment details.

Tracking and ads

No ads, no analytics, no tracking. No data is shared with third parties.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Delete a memory inside the app, use Settings → Delete everything, or delete the app.
