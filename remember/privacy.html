Remember

Privacy Policy. Last updated: November 9, 2026

Remember keeps the notes, recordings, photos, documents and links you save, and answers questions about them. This policy explains what happens to that information.

What we collect

Nothing. We don't run servers for this app, there are no accounts, and we can't see anything you save.

Where your memories are stored

On your iPhone, inside the app's own storage. They are included in your iPhone backup (encrypted if your backup is). They are not synced to any cloud service by the app.

How answers are made

Searching, reading text in photos and documents, and finding names all use Apple frameworks that run on the iPhone. Written answers and summaries come from Apple's on-device language model (Apple Intelligence), which runs on the iPhone and does not send your memories to Apple or to us.

Microphone and speech recognition

The microphone is used only while you record a voice note. After a recording, the app turns it into text with Apple's speech recognition. On most iPhones this happens on the device. For some languages Apple may process the audio on Apple's servers under Apple's privacy policy. You can turn speech recognition off for Remember in iPhone Settings; recordings still work without it.

Calendar

Off unless you turn it on. When on, the app reads your events from the last 90 days and the next 60 so you can ask about them. Events are copied into the app on your iPhone and never sent anywhere.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you scan paper. Photos are read only when you pick them or share them to Remember.

Share extension

When you share something to Remember from another app, it is saved into the app's storage on your iPhone. Nothing else is read from the other app.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time purchase are processed by Apple. We receive no payment details.

Tracking and ads

No ads, no analytics, no tracking. No data is shared with third parties.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Delete a memory inside the app, use Settings → Delete everything, or delete the app.
