Getting it in takes seconds.

Say it.Tap the mic on your Lock Screen and talk on the walk back to the truck. It writes down every word.
Snap it.Receipts, measurements on a 2x4, the whiteboard at the supplier. The text in the photo becomes searchable.
Screenshot it.Customer texts, order confirmations, delivery windows. Bring in your recent screenshots in one tap.
Share it.Estimates, contracts and emails. Tap Share, then Remember, from any app.
