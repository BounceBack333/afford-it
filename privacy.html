Can We Afford It? Budget

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Can We Afford It? is a budgeting app. This policy explains what happens to the information you put in it.

What we collect

Nothing. We don't run servers for this app, we don't have accounts, and we can't see your budget.

Where your information is stored

Everything you enter (income, bills, spending, goals and names) is stored on your iPhone. If you're signed in to iCloud, it's also stored in your own private iCloud account so it can sync between your devices. Apple handles that storage under Apple's privacy policy.

If you invite a partner, the budget you share is stored in iCloud and visible to the people who join it. Anyone who has the invite link can join, so send it only to your partner. The shared budget also stores the date the subscription is paid through, so one subscription can cover you both. You can stop sharing at any time from Plan → Stop sharing, and a partner can leave from Plan → Leave this budget.

Bank accounts

The app does not connect to banks and never asks for bank logins or account numbers.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We receive no payment details. The app checks with Apple whether your subscription is active.

Tracking and ads

The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not share data with third parties.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove data from your phone. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, and delete the app's data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
