Common questions

How do I share a budget with my partner?
Go to Plan, set Budget for to Us two, then tap Invite your partner. They open the link on their iPhone and you both see the same budget. Both of you need to be signed in to iCloud.

Does my partner need their own subscription?
No. Once your partner joins your shared budget, one subscription covers both of you, whichever of you pays.

How do I log Apple Pay purchases with Shortcuts?
Go to Plan, then Siri and Apple Pay, and follow the short setup. Purchases from stores that match a bucket are logged there. Stores it doesn't recognize, like groceries or gas, are skipped unless you pick a bucket for them in Plan. Review the first few logs, and add any missed purchases manually.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?
Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Apple handles all billing.

Do you connect to my bank?
No. The app never asks for bank logins or account numbers.

How do I delete my data?
Go to Plan, then Delete this budget. If you joined your partner's budget, use Plan, See who's in it, or leave instead. You can also delete the app and remove its iCloud data in Settings, your name, iCloud, Manage Account Storage.
