Common questions

Can I measure a layout on the map? Yes. Look up the job address, draw each fence run on the satellite map and use those lengths in the estimate. Map measurements are approximate; check them on site before quoting or ordering.

Where do lot lines work? Lot lines come from public county and state maps, and the app only uses maps whose published terms allow use in an app like this one. Today that is Arkansas, Colorado, Connecticut, Washington DC, Indiana, Maryland, Minnesota (participating counties), Mississippi, New Jersey, Utah and Vermont, plus Los Angeles, Sacramento and Santa Clara counties in California, Fulton and DeKalb counties in Georgia, Lake County in Illinois, Jefferson County in Kentucky, East Baton Rouge Parish in Louisiana and Sarpy County in Nebraska. Each outline shows where it came from. Everywhere else, draw the fence on the satellite photo.

Why are there no prices in the app? Every number on your quotes should be yours. Material prices start empty and labor rates start at zero. Each item links to a real product page so you can check today's price at your store and enter what you pay.

What is the 3-day cancellation notice? The FTC Cooling-Off Rule gives customers 3 business days to cancel a sale of $25 or more made at their home. When it's on in Settings, signed estimates include the notice and the cancellation form. Your state may have its own rules too.

Are the lot lines exact? No. They come from assessor maps, not a survey. Find the property pins or get a survey before fencing a property line.

Can I work without a signal? Quotes and saved job records work locally. Address lookup and satellite maps need an internet connection. Lot lines and crew-day weather from the National Weather Service also need a connection.

How do I back up my jobs? Settings → Backup → Back up all jobs. Save the file to iCloud Drive or email it to yourself. The JSON file includes jobs, settings and signatures, but does not include job photos. Keep a separate copy of the Photos folder from Files → On My iPhone → Fence Estimate, or use a full iPhone backup with this app included.

Does the app clear a job for digging? No. Record the exact dates from your official 811 ticket and check every required utility response and the site markings. Planning guidance does not account for every holiday or special rule and never replaces 811.
