Fence Estimate privacy

We do not operate a server that receives your jobs or use advertising or analytics SDKs in Fence Estimate. The app does not require an account. The map, parcel and weather services described below receive requests when you use those features.

What stays on your phone

Jobs, customers, notes, photos, prices and settings are saved on your iPhone. Your iPhone's own iCloud Backup may include them. If you make a backup file, you decide where it goes. The exported JSON includes jobs, settings and signatures; job photos remain separate in the app’s Photos folder.

What the app sends to show maps, lot lines and weather

  • Apple Maps gets the address you look up and the map area you view.
  • County and state parcel map servers get the map point of the property, to return the lot outline.
  • The National Weather Service gets the map point of a scheduled job, to return the forecast.

These requests go directly to the service, not to us. They also expose ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, to that service. Each service has its own privacy policy and retention practices.

Links you open

Store product links and 811 links open in Safari. Those sites have their own policies.

Support email

If you email support, we receive the email address and any information or attachments you choose to send. Please avoid sending customer records unless they are necessary to explain the issue. You can request deletion of your support correspondence by emailing the same address.
